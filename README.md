# Proxy 15

**Free, local Christmas and photo card layouts for home printing.**

Make cards with your family, choose finished card and paper dimensions in inches or millimetres, and prepare PDFs for your own printer. Proxy 15 supports flat and folded cards, automatic or manual duplex workflows, and a labelled calibration sheet.

The first public release is prepared on the [`codex/public-release` branch](https://github.com/tessellariumlabs/proxy15/tree/codex/public-release) for review. That branch contains the complete source, desktop interface, installation instructions, printing guide, and tests.

[Get the code and read the full README](https://github.com/tessellariumlabs/proxy15/tree/codex/public-release#readme) · [View pull requests](https://github.com/tessellariumlabs/proxy15/pulls)

Your artwork stays on your computer. After installing its Python dependencies, Proxy 15 works offline. It creates print files; you choose the paper, duplex mode, and scaling in your printer dialog.
